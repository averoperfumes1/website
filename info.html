Questions

How long does an Avero fragrance last?

Expect eight to twelve hours on skin. Apply to pulse points after moisturizing for the longest wear.

How much does shipping cost, and how long does it take?

Shipping is free on all orders. Delivery takes 3 to 5 working days in Egypt and 5 to 9 days worldwide, in a signed-for gift box.

Can I return a bottle?

Unopened bottles can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Opened bottles can be exchanged if you are not happy with the scent.

Are the bottles refillable?

Yes. Send back an empty Avero bottle and we will refill it at 25% off the original price.
